How It Works

Simple. Timely. Profitable.

Getting onto clean, cheaper energy takes four straightforward steps — typically 6–12 weeks for On-site Solar PV from first call to first delivery. Energy wheeling is dependant on negotiations up to financial close.

01
Initial Conversation

We learn about your site, consumption profile, and goals — understanding whether wheeling, onsite PPA, or an EPC deal is the right fit. No cost, no commitment.

02
Opportunity Match

We identify the right deal type and introduce you to the relevant producer or EPC contractor — wheeling, onsite PPA, or EPC. No negotiation on our side; the deal is yours to conclude.

03
Agreement Concluded

You engage directly with the producer or EPC contractor. The agreement is between you and them — we've done our part by originating the right opportunity.

04
Clean Energy Live

Clean energy flows to your facility via wheeling or your onsite system. The producer or EPC manages delivery — you enjoy lower costs and verified green credentials.
