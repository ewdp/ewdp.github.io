Commercial & Industrial Energy

Powering Businesses Behind the Meter.

Aethel Energy is expanding its commercial and industrial energy offering through behind-the-meter (BTM) solar PV and Battery Energy Storage Systems. We see onsite generation and storage as an important part of the future energy mix for South African businesses.

Our approach combines onsite renewable generation with intelligent energy storage to help facilities manage their electricity consumption, reduce exposure to peak tariffs, improve operational resilience and make better use of their available energy resources.

Peak Shaving Use stored energy to reduce electricity demand during expensive peak periods, subject to the applicable tariff structure.
Load Shifting Store electricity when conditions are favourable and discharge it when the facility requires additional energy.
Solar Optimisation Integrate solar generation and BESS to improve onsite energy utilisation and manage excess generation.
Energy Resilience Support critical operations through appropriately designed storage and backup configurations.

Project feasibility, savings and system configuration depend on site load profiles, tariff structures, connection conditions, equipment specifications and technical assessments.
